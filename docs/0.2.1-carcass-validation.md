## Changed responsibilities

- `client/render/CarcassVisualState`: owned snapshot and one stage/material decoder for blocks, items and carriers.
- `client/render/CarcassPresentation`: measured visible envelopes, torso grips, explicit contexts and bounded caches.
- `client/render/CarcassModels`: cached per-appearance unticked dummies; alternating variants reuse decoded data.
- `client/render/CarcassScene`: restoration starts before posing; head/plaque frames unwind on failure.
- `client/render/CarcassRenderer`: only installed block entities sample world support; detached bodies use a neutral pose.
- `client/pose/PresentationProfile`: validated resource presentation tuning, independent of world anatomy and gameplay.
- `client/mixin/CarcassItemCullingMixin`: item-render frustum bounds, without enlarging entity collision/pickup boxes.
- `client/compat/carryon`, `mixin/compat/CarryOnMixinPlugin`: optional per-player carried rendering and target gating.
- `butcher/CarcassPolicy*`, `def/DeathSpawnMode`, `config/ServerConfig`: independent per-world policies and frozen-policy sync.
- `butcher/CarcassPlacement`, `HangHandler`: protected destination transactions and rollback guards.
- `butcher/PortableCarcass`, `CarcassBlockEntity`, `MobBlockItem`: bounded portable process state and derived hint flags.
- `butcher/Bleeding`, Jade and the floor hint: real hook/rope support is required when ground bleeding is disabled,
  including Carry On placements that retain a serialized hanging pose without any support.
- Data providers and generated models, loot, language, guide pages and advancements follow the same contracts.
- New item/carry smoke galleries, Carry On handler tests, policy tests and hopper/item transport tests extend existing checks.

World anatomy, stage numbering, registration IDs and selection envelopes are preserved. Item presentation does not require
an anatomy revision bump or replacement bounds export. Geometry/targeting regression runs verify this separation.
