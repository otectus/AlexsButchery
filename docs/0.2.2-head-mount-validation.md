# 0.2.2 head-mount validation

## Scope

This release corrects head/trophy placement and measured anatomy. The runtime checks below exercise these fixes;
the subsequent version bump and packaging cleanup do not change their behavior:

- Wall-mounted skulls contact the wood face at 15/16 in all four facings; the previous contact used an assumed surface at
  12/16, leaving a three-pixel rear gap.
- The anchor is the first solid skull/neck cube. Root-owned moose antlers and descendant ears/tusks cannot become the
  anchor. An empty Cosmaw mouth root instead uses all solid descendants.
- `CubeVisibility.measure` measures actual transformed cube corners, including inflated/shrunk quads. This corrects the
  crow, hummingbird and skreecher anatomy. Flat planes do not support loose heads.
- Small trophies use a half-width, half-height regular wall plaque instead of Butchery's tilted tabletop stand. Plaque collision and selection
  include its actual size and lower trim. Head/rug offsets remain local to each facing; reload clears cached head poses.
- The bundled bounds export is revision 5, which denotes solid head contact and wall-plaque geometry.

Source anchors: `HeadMountBlock.FACE` is 15/16 and its plaque shapes define collision; `HeadPose` and `ModelParts`
measure the solid head geometry; `CubeVisibility` reads the actual rendered vertices; `CarcassModels.clear()` clears
`HeadPose`; and `CarcassBounds.GEOMETRY_REVISION` is 5. The targeted client checks are in `HeadContactChecks` and
`GeometryChecks`.
